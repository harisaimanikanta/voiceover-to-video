# Treatment v2: "The Doctor Who Drank the Cure" (one continuous take)

## What v1 got wrong (the owner's review, accepted)
Props weren't attached to hands (the flask ended up on his head). Characters walked through walls. One shot was blank. The camera was static.
Hard cuts everywhere, flat colours/saturation, dull lighting and surfaces, empty dead space. The "realistic" mannequins looked worse than a
consistent cartoon would.

## v2 rules
1. **One take: zero cuts.** The camera never stops. Every scene change happens *through* something (liquid, window light, a lens,
   a mouth, a monitor, paper, gold) at one of the narrator's natural pauses (0.3–0.5 s, measured).
2. **Camera on the voice.** Emphasis peaks (measured RMS per word) get a camera accent: a whip, a push or a reveal.
   Peaks: BARRY 10.61 · MARSHALL 38.23 · ANOTHER 53.51 · crawling 56.53 · piglets 40.83 · really 67.08 · antacids 76.44 · embarrassed 84.89.
3. **Fill the frame.** Foreground depth layers (out-of-focus props), busy set dressing, particles (dust motes, bubbles, sparks, gas, confetti, pages).
4. **Light and colour.** Volumetric beams, glowing emissives (bacteria, broth, acid, medals), bloom, a saturation/contrast grade, warm/cool split toning.
5. **Consistent cartoon cast.** One design per character for the whole film (round-head explainer style), props *parented to hands*, every pose
   checked from the camera angle used.
6. **Automated QA.** Every 0.25 s is checked for blank/flat frames before the final render.
